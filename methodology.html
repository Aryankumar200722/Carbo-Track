TRANSPARENT INSTITUTIONAL INVENTORY

Campus emissions
methodology.

CarboTrack uses activity data multiplied by an emission factor, aggregated by month, facility and GHG scope. This is an inventory management prototype, not an audited or certified reporting system.

Scope 1 · Direct emissions

Fuel combustion in campus generators, owned vehicles and boilers; refrigerant leakage where reliable top-up or leak data is available.

Scope 2 · Purchased energy

Purchased electricity measured from utility bills or sub-meters. Location-based grid factors should match the reporting period. Market-based accounting needs separate contractual data.

Scope 3 · Other indirect emissions

Relevant categories may include waste treatment, purchased goods, commuting, business travel and upstream energy. Scope 3 should be screened for materiality and data availability; it is not complete merely because a few categories are entered.

Calculation

Emissions (kg CO₂e) = Activity quantity × Emission factor (kg CO₂e per unit). The factor value is snapshotted when a record is saved so future factor updates do not silently rewrite historical records.

The starter factor set includes a CEA India grid factor for FY 2025–26. Diesel, petrol, LPG, landfill waste and paper factors are labelled provisional screening assumptions and must be validated/replaced before formal institutional reporting.

Reference: Central Electricity Authority CO₂ Baseline Database. Consult the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 2 Guidance when defining the inventory boundary and accounting treatment.

Data quality requirements

Retain utility bills, fuel invoices, meter logs and waste manifests. Validate units, reporting periods, organisational boundary, completeness, factor provenance and uncertainty. Missing data should be flagged—not silently treated as zero.
