TRANSPARENCY MATTERS

The thinking
behind the numbers.

CarboTrack is an educational screening calculator. It converts user-reported lifestyle activity into indicative annual greenhouse-gas emissions.

Calculation framework

Each category is estimated using an activity measure multiplied by an illustrative emission factor. Results are converted to kilograms of carbon dioxide equivalent (kg CO₂e) and summed across categories.

Important: The factors currently embedded in this starter build are simplified illustrative defaults, not a validated India-specific factor library. Replace them with documented, geographically and temporally appropriate sources before research, institutional reporting or public comparison.

Included categories

TransportDistance × frequency × mode factor, plus a simplified flight estimate.
Home energyElectricity and LPG use multiplied by illustrative factors.
FoodDiet-pattern proxy multiplied by 365 days.
WaterDaily direct water-use proxy. This is not a full water lifecycle inventory.
WasteWeekly waste estimate multiplied by a simplified factor.
ShoppingMonthly spend proxy. Actual embodied emissions vary by product and supply chain.

Limitations and future validation

  • Household allocation, occupancy and shared transport may affect individual attribution.
  • Food, shopping and waste categories require more granular activity data for robust estimates.
  • Emission factors should cite authoritative sources, version dates, geography and units.
  • Uncertainty ranges should be reported rather than implying false precision.
  • Formal institutional inventories should follow a recognized standard and independent review.

Useful references for future factor validation include the IPCC Guidelines, national grid emission-factor publications and the GHG Protocol. Consult the latest applicable editions and document each factor used.
