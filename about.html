ABOUT THE PROJECT

Make sustainability
easier to understand.

CarboTrack is a campus-oriented educational platform designed to help individuals understand the estimated climate impact of everyday lifestyle choices.

Our purpose

The platform turns everyday activity data into an accessible carbon footprint estimate, explains the major contributing categories and suggests practical actions. It is intended as a learning and awareness tool, not a substitute for a verified greenhouse-gas inventory.

What the platform offers

  • Personal footprint estimation across six lifestyle categories.
  • Visual breakdowns and reduction recommendations.
  • Personal dashboard and a seven-day green habit challenge.
  • Optional account and campus leaderboard infrastructure.
  • Transparent methodology and privacy-first design.

Technology

HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS utilities, Chart.js, Supabase and PostgreSQL. The static calculator works without an account; cloud functionality requires Supabase configuration.
