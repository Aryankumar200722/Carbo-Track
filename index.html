GHG PROTOCOL-ALIGNED STRUCTURE

See the full emissions picture.

Organise the inventory into direct, purchased-energy and other indirect emissions. Define boundaries before comparing performance.

SCOPE 01

Direct emissions

Sources owned or controlled by the institution.

  • Diesel generator sets
  • Institution-owned vehicles
  • LPG and stationary fuels
  • Refrigerant leakage
SCOPE 02

Purchased energy

Indirect emissions from acquired energy.

  • Grid electricity consumption
  • Purchased steam or cooling
  • Building and sub-meter readings
  • Location-based reporting
SCOPE 03

Other indirect

Relevant value-chain emissions beyond the campus boundary.

  • Waste treatment
  • Purchased goods and paper
  • Commuting and business travel
  • Selected supply-chain categories
