SECURE INSTITUTIONAL ACCESS

One campus.
Clearly assigned access.

Administrators manage the institution. Department staff sign in with their own private staff ID and PIN.

First-time setup: create the administrator account here. This is the only account that can create staff IDs and manage all departments.

Your administrator creates your staff ID and gives you your initial PIN privately. Never share your PIN. Five incorrect attempts temporarily lock the login.

Staff accounts are individual. The administrator's password is never shared with department staff. Your department permissions are enforced by the database.
